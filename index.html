雪痕隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

一句话

雪痕不收集你的任何个人信息。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计或分析工具，也不追踪你。你存下的内容只在你自己的设备和你自己的 iCloud 里，开发者看不到，也没有服务器去接收。

你存的内容放在哪

  • 帖子、文章、图片、评论、你写的想法和高亮，都存成普通文件，放在你选的库文件夹里（这台设备上，或你的 iCloud 云盘里）。
  • 放在 iCloud 云盘时，同步由 Apple 完成，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
  • 删掉 App、删掉帖子，或者直接在「文件」App、访达里删库文件夹，内容就没了。

什么时候会联网

  • 只在你存入、刷新某个链接时，雪痕从你的设备直接访问这个链接所在的网站，下载正文、图片和评论。这和你用浏览器打开那个网页一样，对方网站会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • 有些链接要经过下面的公开接口才能取到内容，链接会发给它们：X 官方的嵌入接口（cdn.syndication.twimg.com）和第三方的 FxTwitter（api.fxtwitter.com）用于 X 帖子；哔哩哔哩和 YouTube 的公开接口用于视频信息；原网页打不开时，会去互联网档案馆（archive.org）查有没有存档。
  • 部分链接会用 Apple 系统自带的链接预览来取标题和封面。
  • 这些网站和服务按它们各自的隐私政策处理请求。雪痕不会把你的库内容、设备信息或任何标识发给它们。

只在设备上处理的功能

识别图片里的文字、AI 总结和标签建议，都在你的设备上完成（用的是 Apple 系统自带的识别和设备端模型），内容不上传。

剪贴板

回到雪痕时，App 只让系统判断剪贴板里的东西像不像网址，不读取内容。你点「存入」才会真正读取。可以在设置里关掉。

照片

只在你点「存到相册」时请求「添加到照片」的权限，用来写入图片和视频。雪痕不读取你的相册。

分享扩展和小组件

分享扩展把你分享过来的链接存进同一个库。小组件只读取 App 在本机留下的最近几条标题和封面，不联网。

订阅和购买

  • 雪痕会员通过 Apple 的 App 内购买开通，付款、续期和退款都由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。开发者拿不到你的付款信息、Apple ID 或姓名。
  • App 只在本机向 Apple 查询你是否开通了会员，用来决定能不能继续新存内容；每月免费条数的计数也只存在这台设备上。

儿童

雪痕不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

这份政策有改动时，会更新本页日期，并随 App 更新一起发布。

Snowmark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short

Snowmark does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. What you save stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it and runs no server that receives it.

Where your saved content lives

  • Posts, articles, images, comments, and your notes and highlights are saved as plain files in the library folder you choose, either on this device or in your iCloud Drive.
  • If the folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple handles syncing under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Deleting a post, deleting the app, or deleting the library folder in Files or Finder removes the content.

When the app goes online

  • Only when you save or refresh a link. Snowmark then fetches the page text, images and comments directly from that website, the same way a browser would, so the website sees your IP address.
  • Some links need these public endpoints, which receive the link: X's embed endpoint (cdn.syndication.twimg.com) and the third-party FxTwitter service (api.fxtwitter.com) for X posts; Bilibili and YouTube public endpoints for video details; and the Internet Archive (archive.org) to look for an archived copy when the original page is gone.
  • For some links the app uses Apple's built-in link preview to get a title and cover image.
  • These sites and services handle requests under their own privacy policies. Snowmark never sends them your library content, device information, or any identifier.

On-device features

Text recognition in images, AI summaries, and tag suggestions run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks and on-device model. Nothing is uploaded.

Clipboard

When you return to the app, it only asks the system whether the clipboard holds something that looks like a web link, without reading it. The content is read only when you tap Save. You can turn this off in Settings.

Photos

The app asks for add-only Photos access when you tap Save to Photos, to write images and videos. It never reads your photo library.

Share extension and widget

The share extension saves links you share into the same library. The widget only reads the latest few titles and covers that the app keeps on the device, and does not go online.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Snowmark membership is purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment, renewal and refunds under Apple's privacy policy. The developer never receives your payment details, Apple ID or name.
  • The app checks with Apple on your device whether you have an active membership, to decide whether new saves are allowed. The monthly free-save count is stored only on your device.

Children

Snowmark is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the change will ship with an app update.
